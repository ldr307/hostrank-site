Servers billed by the hour

Hourly billing means you pay for the hours the server exists, up to the monthly price shown. There is usually no contract term and no renewal date.

A powered-off server can still be billed until it is deleted. Check the provider's billing page before you count on turning it off to stop charges.

Before you pay

  • Check which term the first-term price needs. It often applies only when you prepay a longer term.
  • If you will keep the site longer than one term, compare renewal prices, not just first-term prices.
  • Confirm the final price, taxes included, on the official site before you pay.
