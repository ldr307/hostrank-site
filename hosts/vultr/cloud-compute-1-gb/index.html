    Right after signing up, note the billing date and the renewal price shown in the verified table. Add both to your cost tracking so the recurring charge is visible in reviews. Confirm on the provider's site how cancellation and partial months are billed.

  2. STEP 2Connect your domain

    Point your domain to the server, either by updating nameservers or by adding DNS records at your registrar. After the change, check which nameservers the domain uses, replacing example.com with your own domain: dig +short NS example.com
