Your goal is to run a WordPress blog without managing a server. Practical setup guide

  1. STEP 1Point your domain to the server

    At your domain registrar, update the nameservers or DNS records so your domain points to your new server, using the details the provider gives you. DNS changes can take time to spread. To see which nameservers your domain uses right now, replace example.com with your own domain and run this from your computer: dig +short NS example.com

  2. STEP 2Enable an SSL certificate

    Your blog should load over HTTPS so visitors and search engines see a secure connection. The official data does not confirm that an SSL certificate is included, so check how to get and install one on this plan. Once it is set up, replace example.com with your domain and confirm that HTTPS responds by running: curl -I https://example.com

  3. STEP 3Find out how backups and restores work

    The official data does not mention backups, so confirm whether they are offered, whether they cost extra and how a restore works. Until you know, keep your own copies of your WordPress files and database. Test a restore once so you know your blog can come back after a problem.

  4. STEP 4Note your renewal date and renewal price

    The official data lists a term of one month and a flat monthly price with no intro discount. Write down when billing renews and check the renewal price in the table on this page. A reminder before each renewal makes it easier to review whether the plan still fits your blog.
