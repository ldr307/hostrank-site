What TMDHosting Starter includes

TMDHosting Starter is a Shared Hosting plan from TMDHosting. The official pricing page lists these features: 1 Website, Standard Performance, Free SSL Certificates, No Backups, Free Domain, Free Domain Transfer, No Setup Fee, Free Website Transfer, LiteSpeed Web Server, Free cPanel, Free CloudFlare CDN, SSH and SFTP Access, and a 30 day money back guarantee.

The listed storage is 50GB SSD. Bandwidth is not listed for this plan, so store owners who expect traffic spikes from sales or promotions should ask TMDHosting about traffic limits before signing up.

Who this plan suits

The plan fits a store owner running a single shop on one site, since the feature list says 1 Website. Owners who plan to add a second storefront, a separate blog domain or a staging copy on its own domain may need a plan that allows more websites.

Free Website Transfer and Free Domain Transfer can help an owner who already runs a shop elsewhere and wants to move it. The feature list does not name specific e-commerce software, so confirm with TMDHosting that the store platform you use is supported.

HTTPS on checkout pages

Customers enter addresses and payment details at checkout, so every store page should load over HTTPS. The plan lists Free SSL Certificates, which covers the certificate itself. After the certificate is active, open the cart and checkout pages in a browser and look for the padlock, and make sure the plain HTTP address redirects to HTTPS.

Free CloudFlare CDN is also listed. If you turn it on, check that HTTPS still works on checkout pages afterward, since a CDN sits between visitors and the server.

Backups: the key gap for a store

The feature list for this plan says No Backups. For a store, that means orders, product listings, customer accounts and theme changes are not protected by a hosting backup on this plan. Plan your own backup routine from the first day, and keep copies somewhere other than the hosting account.

The plan lists SSH and SFTP Access and Free cPanel, which give ways to download site files and databases. Many store platforms also offer export tools or backup plugins. Whichever method you choose, test a restore on a copy of the site so you know the backup actually works.

TMDHosting Business, covered below, lists 30 Backup Copies, which may matter to owners who would rather not manage backups alone.

Billing and renewal to check before signing up

Billing on the official pricing page is described as USD per month, paid triennially, with a term of 36 months. In practice, this means the full term is paid up front rather than month by month, so check the total due at checkout in the price table.

Practical setup guide

  1. STEP 1Set up or transfer the store

    Install your store or request the Free Website Transfer listed for the plan, and keep the current site live while the new copy is prepared. Check products, cart and checkout on the new hosting before any visitors are sent to it. Confirm your store software is supported if you have not already.

  2. STEP 2Connect the domain

    Once the store works on the new hosting, point the domain at it by updating nameservers or DNS records at your registrar, or use the Free Domain or Free Domain Transfer listed for the plan. DNS changes can take some time to reach all visitors, so avoid editing orders or products on the old site during the switch.

  3. STEP 3Turn on HTTPS for checkout

    After the domain points at the new hosting, activate the Free SSL Certificates included with the plan. Then check the response headers from your own computer, replacing example.com with your domain: curl -I https://example.com. Also open checkout pages in a browser and make sure HTTP redirects to HTTPS.

  4. STEP 4Create your own backup routine

    Because the plan lists No Backups, set up your own backups right away using SSH and SFTP Access, cPanel or your store platform's export tools. Over SSH, an archive of the site files can be made from the home directory, replacing public_html if your files live elsewhere: tar -czf site-backup.tar.gz public_html. Export the database as well, download both copies off the server, and test a restore.

  5. STEP 5Record billing details

    Note the renewal date at the end of the term of 36 months and the renewal price shown in the table. Set a reminder well before that date so you can decide whether to renew or switch. Keep the money back guarantee conditions on file in case the plan is not a fit.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does TMDHosting Starter include SSL for a checkout page?

    Yes, the plan lists Free SSL Certificates. After setup, confirm that cart and checkout pages load over HTTPS and that HTTP addresses redirect to HTTPS.

  • Does TMDHosting Starter back up my store?

    No. The feature list says No Backups, so the store owner is responsible for making and testing backups. TMDHosting Business lists 30 Backup Copies if hosting backups are a priority.

  • How many stores can run on this plan?

    The plan lists 1 Website. Owners who need more sites can compare TMDHosting Business, which lists Unlimited Websites, or the Namecheap plans described above.

  • How is the plan billed?

    It is billed in USD per month and paid triennially, with a term of 36 months. See the price table for the amount due and the renewal price.

  • Can an existing store be moved to TMDHosting Starter?

    The plan lists Free Website Transfer and Free Domain Transfer. Ask TMDHosting how the transfer works for your store platform before scheduling the move.
