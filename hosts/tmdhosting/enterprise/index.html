What TMDHosting Enterprise includes

TMDHosting Enterprise is a shared hosting plan from TMDHosting, which means the provider manages the server and you manage your website. The listed storage is Unlimited SSD. The official features are: Unlimited Websites, Performance x4, Free SSL Certificates, 30 Backup Copies, Free Domain, Free Domain Transfer, No Setup Fee, Free Website Transfer, LiteSpeed Web Server, Free cPanel, Free CloudFlare CDN, SSH and SFTP Access, 99.9% Uptime Guarantee, and 30 day money back guarantee.

For a business owner, several of these line up directly with launch tasks. Free Domain and Free Domain Transfer relate to getting your company name online, Free SSL Certificates relate to HTTPS, 30 Backup Copies relate to recovering from mistakes, and Free Website Transfer is relevant if you are moving an existing site from another host. Free cPanel gives a familiar hosting control panel, and SSH and SFTP Access helps if you or a developer prefer working with files and tools directly.

The official data does not list CPU, RAM or bandwidth for this plan, and it does not mention email hosting. If company email addresses on your own domain matter to you, confirm with TMDHosting whether email is included before signing up.

Who this plan suits

Unlimited Websites makes this plan relevant for a business that expects to run more than one site, such as a main company site plus a separate landing site, a booking site or a site for a second brand. Performance x4 is the highest performance label among the TMDHosting plans listed on this page, which may matter if you expect your site to grow.

If you only need a single small brochure site and do not care about backups, the lighter TMDHosting plans described below may fit your needs. If backups and room for several sites matter, Enterprise and Business both include 30 Backup Copies and Unlimited Websites.

What to check before signing up

Billing: the plan is listed in USD per month and is paid triennially (36 months). In practice that means you commit to a 36 months term paid up front rather than paying month to month. Check the price table for the amount due today and what it covers.

Renewal: the official data does not state whether the renewal price is higher, lower or the same as the introductory price. Read the renewal price and term in the price table, and confirm them with TMDHosting before you pay, since a 36 months term is a long commitment for a small business budget.

Included extras: No Setup Fee and the 30 day money back guarantee are listed features. Check the terms of the money back guarantee, including whether the Free Domain is treated differently if you cancel.

Backups: 30 Backup Copies is listed, but the data does not explain how often copies are made or how restores are requested. Find out how restoring works before you need it.

Practical setup guide

  1. STEP 1Build or transfer the site first

    Before changing anything about your domain, get the website ready on the new hosting. If you are moving an existing site, Free Website Transfer is a listed feature, so request it and wait until the copy is complete. Keep your old host active so visitors still reach your current site in the meantime.

  2. STEP 2Point your domain at the new hosting

    Once the site is ready, connect your domain by changing its nameservers or DNS records to the values TMDHosting gives you, or use the Free Domain or Free Domain Transfer features. To see which nameservers the domain uses now, run this from your computer, replacing example.com with your domain: dig +short NS example.com. Changes can take some time to spread, so do not cancel the old host yet.

  3. STEP 3Enable HTTPS

    After the domain points to the new hosting, turn on the SSL certificate, since Free SSL Certificates is a listed feature. Then load your site in a browser and confirm the padlock appears on both the plain domain and the www version. Fix any pages that still load images or scripts over plain HTTP.

  4. STEP 4Check backups and email

    The plan lists 30 Backup Copies, so find out how often copies are taken and how to restore one, and consider keeping your own copy of important files too. Also set up and test company email addresses, or arrange another email service if TMDHosting confirms email is not part of the plan.

  5. STEP 5Record your billing details

    Note the start date of your 36 months term, the renewal date and the renewal price shown in the price table. Add a reminder ahead of the renewal date so you can review the plan in time. Also note the deadline of the 30 day money back guarantee in case the plan does not suit your business.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does TMDHosting Enterprise include a domain for my business?

    Free Domain is a listed feature, and Free Domain Transfer is listed for a domain you already own. Check the conditions attached to the free domain, such as what happens at renewal or if you cancel.

  • Will my business site have HTTPS?

    Free SSL Certificates is a listed feature. After your domain points to the new hosting, enable the certificate and confirm HTTPS works by running this from your own computer, replacing example.com with your domain: curl -I https://example.com

  • Is business email included?

    Email is not mentioned in the plan data, so ask TMDHosting directly before signing up if you need addresses on your own domain.

  • How is the plan billed?

    It is listed in USD per month and paid triennially (36 months), so the full term is paid up front. The price table shows the current amount and the renewal price.

  • Can I move my existing website?

    Free Website Transfer is a listed feature. Ask what the transfer covers and plan to keep your old site live until the new copy is checked.
