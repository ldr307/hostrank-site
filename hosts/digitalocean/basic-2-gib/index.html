How to judge total cost: intro price, renewal price and term

Total hosting cost depends on three things read together: what you pay at the start, what you pay at renewal, and how long each billing term lasts. On many plans the intro price applies only to the first term, and the renewal price that follows can be noticeably different. A plan that looks inexpensive at signup can cost more over a year than one with a steady rate.

For this plan, the official data states "Flat monthly price (no intro discount)". That wording indicates no introductory discount is offered, which makes forecasting simpler. Even so, check the price and renewal price columns in the table on this page and confirm they match before adding the plan to a budget.

Because the term is 1 month, the plan renews on a monthly cycle. When projecting annual spend, use the renewal price from the table for each month rather than relying on any single figure, and note whether taxes or add-ons apply, since the official data does not list them.
