Who Namecheap Stellar Business suits

This plan is aimed at site owners who want the provider to manage the server. As Shared Hosting, there is no operating system to maintain, no firewall to configure and no server software to patch; the account holder works with the site itself rather than the machine behind it. For a small business owner whose priority is a working company website rather than server administration, that is the main appeal of this category.

The single listed feature, Unlimited websites, matters for a business that may run more than one site: a main company site, a separate landing page for a campaign, or a site for a second trading name. With 50 GB SSD storage and Unmetered bandwidth, the plan is described with resources suited to brochure-style business sites, service pages and a company blog. The listing does not describe performance, so nothing on this page should be read as a statement about speed or uptime.

Resources and features as listed

The official data for Namecheap Stellar Business lists the following: Unlimited websites as the only feature, 50 GB SSD as storage, and Unmetered bandwidth. The term is 12 months. Because the feature list is short, a business owner should treat it as the full set of confirmed facts rather than a summary. Anything beyond Unlimited websites, 50 GB SSD and Unmetered bandwidth needs to be checked on the provider's plan page before relying on it.

How billing and renewal work

This does not mean those items are absent, only that they are not in the verified listing. Before paying, check the provider's plan page for each of them, especially email and backups, since a business depends on both every day.

Launch checks for a business site

Before announcing the site, a business owner should confirm four things in order: the domain resolves to the hosting account, the site loads over HTTPS without warnings, business email sends and receives, and a recent backup exists somewhere other than the hosting account itself. The setup guide below walks through these in a working order.

A quick way to confirm the domain is using the nameservers from the hosting account is to run this from a laptop, replacing example.com with the company domain: dig +short NS example.com. If the output still shows the old nameservers, the change at the registrar has not taken effect yet or was not saved.

Practical setup guide

  1. STEP 1Point the company domain at the hosting account

    After signup, locate the nameservers or DNS details the hosting account provides and enter them at the domain registrar. If the business already has a live site elsewhere, keep that site running until the new one is complete and then change the records. The dig command in the launch checks section confirms when the new nameservers are in use.

  2. STEP 2Enable HTTPS once the domain resolves

    When the domain points at the new account, enable an SSL certificate using whichever option the hosting account provides, as noted in the section on unconfirmed items. Set the site to redirect plain HTTP to HTTPS so customers never land on an insecure version. Confirm the result from a laptop, replacing example.com with the company domain: curl -I https://example.com.

  3. STEP 3Set up business email and a first backup

    Having checked how email is handled for this plan, create the mailboxes or forwarding the business needs and send a test message in each direction before publishing the address on the site. Then find out how backups and restores work in the account and take a manual copy of the site files and database before launch. Store that copy somewhere outside the hosting account so a restore is possible even if the account is unavailable.

  4. STEP 4Record the term, renewal date and renewal price

    Write down the renewal date that follows the 12 months term and the renewal price shown in the price table, since it is higher than the intro price. Confirm at checkout how the prepayment is arranged, because the billing term and prepayment depend on plan. Add the renewal date to the business calendar so the site does not lapse unexpectedly.
