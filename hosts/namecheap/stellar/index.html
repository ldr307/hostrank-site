Checking your blog after launch

You do not need server access to run a few basic checks from your own computer. After you point your domain to the new host, you can see which nameservers it currently uses by running this command and replacing example.com with your own domain: dig +short NS example.com

Once your SSL certificate is active, you can confirm that your blog loads over HTTPS and check for redirects by running this command, again replacing example.com with your domain: curl -I https://example.com
