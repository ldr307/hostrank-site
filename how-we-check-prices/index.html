How we check prices

Where the prices on this site come from and when they were checked.

How a price is checked

1. We open the provider's official pricing page.

2. We copy each plan's first-term price, renewal price, contract term, billing basis (monthly, hourly and so on), currency and specs.

3. We record the check date and show it on every page that uses the price.

Old prices

Plans whose price check is older than 60 days are not published again until they are rechecked.

When a recheck finds a different price, the earlier checked price stays in the plan page's "Price history" table.

Before you pay

Hosting prices change often, and taxes, exchange rates and promotion terms can change what you actually pay. Always confirm the final price on the official site before you pay.
