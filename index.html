How we check prices

Every price on this site was taken by a person from the provider's official pricing page, and each page shows the date it was checked.

Hosting prices change often. When a plan lists a first-term price below its renewal price, the first-term price usually applies only to the contract term shown. Always confirm the final price on the official site before you pay.
