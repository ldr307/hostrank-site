About

hosts.fasthosting.info tracks web hosting and cloud server prices and terms, checked by hand on each provider's official pricing page.

What we do

For each plan we list the first-term price, renewal price, contract term, billing basis and specs, and we compare plans of the same type side by side.

Every price and spec is copied by a person from the provider's official pricing page, and each page shows the date it was checked.

What we don't do

No guessed numbers, no fake reviews and no made-up first-hand test stories.

Plans whose price check is older than 60 days are not published again until they are rechecked.

How pages are written

Page text is drafted with AI from the checked price data and goes through automated quality checks before it is published. Prices and specs come only from the checked data.

How we make money

Some links are affiliate links. If you sign up through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions never change the prices or comparisons we show.
